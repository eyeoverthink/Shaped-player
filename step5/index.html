Starting the outline model

Shaped player · Step 5 of 5

You, live, with no box.

Press Start camera. Your webcam picture is cut out as it arrives and placed on this page as a shape you can drag around. The picture never leaves your device.

What happens when you start

Your browser asks for permission to use the camera. After you allow it, each frame goes through the same person-finding model as step 2, and only you are drawn.

Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. Press Stop camera and the camera light goes off.

Getting a clean outline

Face a window or a lamp so you are brighter than the room behind you. A plain wall helps but is not required. If your desk or chair gets picked up, use Trim bottom.

Mirror flips the picture so it moves the way a mirror does. Turn it off if you are holding up something with writing on it.

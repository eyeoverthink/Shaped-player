Starting the outline model

Shaped player · Step 2 of 4

Any clip, cut out in your browser.

The clip playing in the corner is an ordinary rectangular video. This page finds the person in every frame as it plays and draws only them. Nothing was prepared ahead of time.

Press Choose a clip to try one of your own. It stays on your device; nothing is uploaded.

What changed since step 1

In step 1 the outline was made beforehand and shipped inside the video file. Here the file has no outline in it. A small person-finding model runs on this page, looks at each frame, and hands back the shape.

The same controls work: drag him, press Show the box to see the untouched rectangle, change the background.

Using your own clip

A clip of one person, head and shoulders or full body, works best. The model was trained on people on camera, so plain video calls and talking-head footage come out cleanest.

If something under the person gets picked up, such as a caption bar or the edge of a player, use Trim bottom to cut it off.

What to expect from the edge

This outline is made live, many times a second, so it is softer than the one in step 1, which had as long as it needed per frame. Fine hair and fast movement are where the difference shows.

The readout under the buttons shows how long each frame takes on this device.

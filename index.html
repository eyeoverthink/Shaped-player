Video without the rectangle

Put a person on your page, not a player.

Shaped Player shows video in the shape of whoever is in it. The page shows through everywhere else: no frame, no background, no corners.

For site owners

Two lines of HTML.

Add the script, add the tag, point it at a clip. Choose a corner or set it inside your layout, and decide whether visitors can drag the figure and switch the sound on.

Clicks follow the shape, so links and text beside the figure keep working.

<script src="shaped-player.js"></script>
<shaped-player src="your-clip.mp4"
  corner="bottom-right" width="320"
  movable controls></shaped-player>

Where it fits

Anywhere a talking head gets a box today.

  • CreatorsAn avatar or host standing on the page beside the work, in the page's own colours.
  • ShopsA presenter on a product page who talks without covering the product in a video frame.
  • CoursesA teacher in the corner of the lesson, over the slides instead of beside them.
  • StreamsA webcam cut-out with no green screen and no rectangle.

How it works

One ordinary video file carries its own outline.

Each frame holds two pictures: the colour image on top and a black and white outline of the figure underneath. The player reads both and draws only what the outline allows.

Because it is a plain H.264 MP4, it plays where transparent video formats do not, including iPhone.

  • Script12 KB, no other libraries
  • ClipOne MP4, picture and outline together
  • OutlineMade in the browser by a person-finding model, or offline for a sharper edge
  • PrivacyConversion and live camera run on the device; nothing is sent to a server
